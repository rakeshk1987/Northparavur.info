North Paravur is the headquarters of Paravur Taluk in Ernakulam district, located just 17 km north of Kochi city.

Paravur Assembly Constituency

Kalamassery Assembly Constituency (Includes Eloor Municipality, Alangad, Kadungalloor, Karumalloor, and Kunnukara)

North Paravur Municipality: One of the oldest municipalities in Ernakulam district and the administrative headquarters of Paravur Taluk. The ancient seaport of Muziris is located in Pattanam village just 3 km away from town.

Eloor Municipality: An industrial island suburb of Kochi located on the Periyar River within Paravur Taluk, around 13 km north of Kochi city centre.
